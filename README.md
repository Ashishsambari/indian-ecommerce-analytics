# 🛒 Indian E-commerce Analytics | End-to-End Data Analytics Project


**Turning Raw E-commerce Data into Actionable Business Insights**







---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Indian E-commerce Analytics** project is an end-to-end data analytics solution designed to analyze e-commerce business performance, customer purchasing patterns, product profitability, and regional sales trends.

The project combines **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to transform raw datasets into meaningful business insights through data inspection, validation, business analysis, and interactive dashboards.

The primary objective is to understand business performance and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- 📊 Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- 👥 Understand customer purchasing behavior and segmentation.
- 📦 Identify high-performing and low-performing product categories.
- 💰 Evaluate product profitability and profit margins.
- 🌍 Analyze regional revenue distribution across Indian states.
- 📈 Identify monthly sales trends and business patterns.
- 🔍 Perform data quality checks and validate dataset relationships.

## 📊 Power BI Dashboard

The project includes three dashboard pages designed to provide different perspectives of e-commerce business performance.

### 1️⃣ Executive Overview

Provides a high-level overview of business performance.

**Key Analysis:**

- Total customers
- Total orders
- Gross order value
- Monthly revenue trends
- Orders by month
- Revenue by customer segment



### 2️⃣ Product & Profitability

Analyzes product category performance and profitability.

**Key Analysis:**

- Revenue by product category
- Total units sold
- Overall profit
- Profit by category
- Product-level profitability
- Category-based filtering



### 3️⃣ Customer Analytics

Focuses on customer behavior and regional performance.

**Key Analysis:**

- Customer distribution by segment
- Average orders
- Revenue by state
- Customer segment comparison
- Year-based filtering
